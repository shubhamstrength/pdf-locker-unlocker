PDF Locker

Add or remove a PDF password. Everything happens in your browser, so your files are never uploaded.

    Restrict printing, copying or editing
    Needed later to lift the restrictions. Must differ from the open password.

    Unlocking makes a copy without a password or restrictions. Leave the password empty for PDFs that open freely but block printing or copying.

    Uses qpdf compiled to WebAssembly, with AES-256 encryption. Prefer a native app? Get PDF Locker for Mac.
